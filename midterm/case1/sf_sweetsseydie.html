Ghost Smore's Cookies

by Seydie Wis

A spooky treat to make during the Halloween season. Sweet chocolate chip cookies with a summer twist that brings you back to the campfire but with a fall taste. (12 servings).

Ingredients

  • Nestle pre-made cookie dough tub
  • Hershey's chocolate chip bars
  • Bag of Marshmallows
  • Graham crackers
  • Edible black marker

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (176° C).
  2. Prep your baking sheet with parchment paper or aluminum baking sheet.
  3. Break graham crackers in half for one portion and lay flat on baking sheet.
  4. Put Hershey's chocolate on top of the graham cracker.
  5. Arrange marshmallows on top to cover the chocolate. Cut in half if needed so that it doesn't fall over or the marshmallow piece is too big.
  6. Round cookie dough into 3-inch balls and then flatten. Drape cookie dough over the top so that it covers the chocolate and leaves room to see the grahram cracker.
  7. Bake for 20-30 minutes or until fully cooked and golden brown. Once done, take out from oven
  8. Grab an additonal marshmallow and draw two eyes and a mouth using the edible black marker. Then place over the melted marshamallow so it sticks. Let it cool, and serve!
